Good Work Has a Next Day

Transcript of the 90-second film. The project shown, Bramble & Bolt, is fictional.

You make something with AI. It looks great. You send it off. Then someone else opens it. A teammate, a customer, or you, next week. And they’re stuck.

They need to know where it came from, why it went this way, what got tested, and where to begin. That’s the part of the work I care about.

You find the question nobody asked. You follow the evidence. You decide who it’s for, and what shape it should take.

Then you build it, and you check what it can do, and where it has to stop.

You make it easier to use. You show it in pictures, in film, and in the product itself.

And you keep the record, so the next person has somewhere to start.

I’ve built a set of AI skills and tools for each of those moments.

Pick the one the work needs. Keep the decisions yours.

Because good work has a next day.

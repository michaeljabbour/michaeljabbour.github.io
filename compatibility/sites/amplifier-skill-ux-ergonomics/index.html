Read the canonical method

UX Ergonomics

Make the task easier to complete, and show what evidence would prove the change helped.

Frame the job, then inspect the surface

Start with task analysis: actor, trigger, desired outcome, context, frequency, expertise, prerequisites, decisions, actions, feedback, interruptions, handoffs, failures, recovery, and completion evidence. Keep nominal flow and exceptions. Do not redesign while still determining the task.

Use evidence synthesis and the evidence ladder to separate direct product behavior, research, standards, inspection, and anecdotes. Record source population, method, setting, date, applicability, contradictions, and uncertainty. Source count is not strength; do not derive prevalence from complaints or social posts.

Review through the relevant lenses

For discoverability, action, feedback, and learning use cognitive walkthrough. State assumed knowledge and distinguish predicted breakdown from observed behavior. For accessibility use layered evaluation: automation, source semantics, keyboard/focus/reflow/motion/timing/error behavior, assistive technology, cognitive access, and affected-user tasks as warranted. A subset cannot establish full conformance.

For agents use authority mapping: information visibility, decision/execution owner, consequence, uncertainty, reversibility, preview/approval/intervention/undo/retry/escalation, completion evidence, and memory/deletion control. Scale friction to the actual risk and existing grant, not a blanket request for confirmation. Consider longitudinal, organizational, and adversarial effects using the retained deeper contexts.

Recommend a falsifiable change

For each consequential finding separate observation, inference, recommendation, and requirement. Name the mechanism, affected task/state, consequence, evidence level, alternatives, uncertainty, and acceptance criterion. Use acceptance criteria; add a numeric threshold only with a baseline, risk, requirement, or decision-power reason. State the method and evidence that would fail the recommendation.

The credited visual craft material preserves distinct source roles and does not require an install. Use it only to serve the task and preserve the author's intentional choices; do not convert a design preference into a measured usability claim.

Verify and learn

Apply adversarial verification, returning pass/revise/blocked with material gaps. Use study design for the lightest valid empirical check: population/variation, tasks/states/environment, behavior measures, assistance/critical errors/abandonment, moderation/counterbalance/access, analysis, stop rule, and generalization bounds. Small formative studies can expose breakdowns; they do not estimate prevalence. Questionnaires supplement behavior.

Return a task-and-evidence map, prioritized findings, authority decisions, concrete changes, acceptance criteria, validation status, and unresolved evidence. An uncalibrated weighted rubric or a structural test is not observed benefit.

Limits and prerequisites
  • Expert inspection is not observed-user evidence; anecdotes cannot establish prevalence.
  • Accessibility conformance needs adequate runtime/manual evidence at the stated scope.
  • Visual Taste guidance is an optional craft lens; it never outranks task evidence, usability, or user voice.

# Ergonomics verification

## Checks

- Every conclusion traces to evidence at the stated level.
- Observation, inference, recommendation, and requirement are distinct.
- Alternative causal explanations were considered.
- Accessibility claims do not exceed tested layers.
- Human-agent authority matches consequence and reversibility.
- Immediate benefits are not confused with longitudinal outcomes.
- Organizational and adversarial failure paths are represented.
- Acceptance criteria are observable and method-compatible.
- Proposed research can actually change the decision.
